
<h1 align="center">Pokémon Silver Perfect Randomizer</h1>

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://files.catbox.moe/5cya6u.png"
       alt="Pokémon Silver Perfect Randomizer Box Art"
       width="400">
</p>

<p align="center">
  A fan-made Pokémon Silver randomizer featuring modernized
  Pokémon stats and moves, BW-style EXP scaling, randomized
  encounters, shuffled overworld items, and quality-of-life improvements.
</p>

---

<h2>✨ Features</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Gen 9 Pokémon stats and move updates.</li>
  <li>Black/White-style Experience Scaling System.</li>
  <li>Randomized three-stage starter evolution lines.</li>
  <li>Randomized wild Pokémon and trainer teams using Similar Strength.</li>
  <li>Shuffled overworld items with a bad-item filter.</li>
  <li>Trade evolution adjustments for single-player progression.</li>
  <li>Universal TM and HM compatibility.</li>
</ul>

<h2>📦 Requirements</h2>

<ul>
  <li>An original Pokémon Silver ROM.</li>
  <li>A compatible patching tool.</li>
  <li>The Pokémon Silver Perfect Randomizer patch.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  <strong>Important:</strong> The original Pokémon Silver ROM
  is not included. You must provide your own legally obtained copy.
</p>
